How it works

  1. Run one small pilot

    Pick one client and one problem you can improve in about two weeks. The free kit gives you a simple plan, so the goal, the measure and the dates are agreed before you start.

  2. Capture what changed

    On day one you write down where things stand, and on the last day you measure the same thing again. Your client signs a consent form at the start, so you never have to chase permission later.

  3. Show the proof

    The before and after figures and your client’s own words go onto one page. You clip it to your next proposal, and the buyer finally has something real to check.

Questions

Do I need clients already?

No. The kit is made for your first pilot, so one willing client is enough. That could be a business you have helped before or someone you already know.

What if my client doesn’t want their name shared?

That is fine. The case study can describe them in general terms, such as “a Sydney physio clinic”, and your client approves the final version before anyone else sees it.

Do I keep my case studies if I cancel?

Yes. Every case study you make is yours to keep, whether you stay for one month or for years.

Can I trust the first drafts in Automate?

The draft only uses the figures you recorded during the pilot. You read and edit it, and your client approves it before it is shared.
